# Employee Management System with RAG AI

A full-stack, role-based platform for modern workforce management featuring an integrated, context-aware AI assistant for policy and leave retrieval.

---

### Key Features

* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Tiered permissions across **Owner**, **HR**, and **Employee** views for secure operations.
* **Leave & Workflows:** Dynamic hierarchical leave requests, approval pipelines, and balance tracking.
* **Company Communications:** Centralized announcements module for company-wide updates.
* **Context-Aware AI Assistant:** RAG-driven chatbot leveraging vector search to answer employee queries regarding internal documents, handbooks, and leave policies.
